Why This Matters in the Kitchen

Tablespoons are one of the most used measurements in cooking — especially for oils, butter, sauces, and spices. Knowing how they relate to cups makes scaling recipes up or down much easier.

Halving and doubling recipes

If a recipe calls for ¼ cup of butter and you want to make half the recipe, you need 2 tablespoons. Since ¼ cup = 4 tablespoons, half of that is 2 tablespoons.

Measuring without a cup

If you don't have a measuring cup handy, tablespoons work perfectly. Just remember: 16 tablespoons = 1 cup, 8 tablespoons = ½ cup, and 4 tablespoons = ¼ cup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many tablespoons in a cup of butter?

16 tablespoons. Most butter sticks are 8 tablespoons (½ cup).

How many tablespoons in ⅓ cup?

5 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon. ⅓ of 16 is 5.333, which equals 5 tablespoons and 1 teaspoon.

How many tablespoons in ¾ cup?

12 tablespoons. Multiply ¾ by 16 to get 12.

How many tablespoons in ⅔ cup?

10 tablespoons plus 2 teaspoons. Two thirds of 16 is 10.667.

Is 6 tablespoons equal to ⅓ cup?

Not exactly. ⅓ cup is 5 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon. 6 tablespoons is closer to ⅜ cup.

How many tablespoons in a cup of sugar?

16 tablespoons. Any dry ingredient uses the same conversion: 1 cup = 16 tablespoons.
