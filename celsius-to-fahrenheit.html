Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 then add 32. Example: 100°C × 9/5 + 32 = 212°F.

What is 180 Celsius in Fahrenheit?

180°C is 356°F — a very common baking temperature.

What is 350°F in Celsius?

350°F is 177°C — the most common baking temperature in the US.

What temperature is the same in Celsius and Fahrenheit?

-40° is the same in both scales!

What is normal body temperature in Celsius?

Normal body temperature is 37°C (98.6°F).
