Why Grams and Cups Don't Convert Simply

Cups measure volume — how much space something takes up. Grams measure weight. A cup of flour weighs much less than a cup of honey because honey is much denser.

The flour problem

Flour is the trickiest ingredient to measure by cup. If you scoop flour directly from the bag, you can pack in up to 50% more than if you spoon it lightly into the cup. This is why professional bakers always weigh flour in grams.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many grams in a cup of flour?

120 grams of all-purpose flour per cup, if measured by spooning flour lightly into the cup and leveling it off. Scooping directly can give you up to 180g.

How many grams in a cup of sugar?

200 grams of white granulated sugar per cup. Brown sugar packed tightly is about 213 grams. Powdered sugar is lighter at about 100 grams per cup.

How many grams in a cup of butter?

227 grams per cup. This equals 2 standard US butter sticks. Half a cup of butter is 114 grams or 1 stick.

How many grams in a cup of rice?

About 185 grams of uncooked white rice per cup. Brown rice is slightly heavier at around 190 grams per cup.

How many grams in a cup of water?

240 grams. Water is one of the few ingredients where grams and milliliters are almost identical — 1ml of water weighs 1 gram.

How many grams in a cup of oats?

About 90 grams of rolled oats per cup. Oats are very light and fluffy so a cup weighs much less than you might expect.
