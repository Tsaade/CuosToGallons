How Many ml in a Tablespoon?

14.79 ml in 1 US tablespoon ≈ 15ml

There are 14.79 milliliters in 1 US tablespoon — commonly rounded to 15ml for cooking. Use the chart and calculator below for any amount.

5ml= 1 teaspoon
15ml≈ 1 tablespoon
30ml= 2 tablespoons

Frequently Asked Questions

How many ml in a tablespoon?

Is 15ml the same as 1 tablespoon?

Almost! 1 tablespoon is 14.79ml, rounded to 15ml for convenience. For cooking this difference is negligible.
