Frequently Asked Questions

How many grams in an ounce?

There are 28.35 grams in 1 ounce.

How many ounces in 100 grams?

100 grams equals 3.53 ounces.

How many grams in 2 ounces?

2 ounces equals 56.7 grams.

How do you convert grams to ounces?

Divide grams by 28.35 to get ounces. Example: 100g ÷ 28.35 = 3.53 oz.

How many grams in a pound?
