How Many Liters in a Gallon?

3.785 L in 1 US gallon

There are 3.785 liters in 1 US gallon. Use the chart and calculator below to convert any amount instantly.

1.893 L= ½ gallon
3.785 L= 1 US gallon
4.546 L= 1 UK gallon
