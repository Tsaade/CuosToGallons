About MeasureUpNow

MeasureUpNow.com is a free kitchen measurement converter built for home cooks, bakers, and anyone who has ever found themselves mid-recipe wondering how many grams are in a cup of flour, or how many tablespoons are in a gallon. We exist to make that answer instant, accurate, and completely free.

Our Mission

Cooking and baking should be enjoyable — not interrupted by confusing unit conversions. Whether you're scaling a recipe up for a crowd, following an international cookbook with metric measurements, or simply trying to figure out how much water to drink each day, MeasureUpNow.com has the answer ready for you.

Every converter on this site is built with simplicity in mind: a clear answer at the top, an interactive calculator, and a full chart for every common amount. No clutter, no pop-ups, no confusion.
