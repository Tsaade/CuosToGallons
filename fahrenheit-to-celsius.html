Fahrenheit to Celsius Converter

(°F − 32) × 5/9 = degrees Celsius

To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius, subtract 32 then multiply by 5/9. Use the calculator and chart below for instant answers.

0°C= 32°F (freezing)
37°C= 98.6°F (body temp)
100°C= 212°F (boiling)
177°C= 350°F (baking)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature, then multiply by 5/9. For example, (212°F − 32) × 5/9 = 100°C.

What is 350 Fahrenheit in Celsius?

350°F equals 177°C, the most common baking temperature.

What is 98.6 Fahrenheit in Celsius?

98.6°F equals 37°C, which is normal human body temperature.

What is 32 Fahrenheit in Celsius?

32°F equals 0°C, the freezing point of water.

What is 100 Fahrenheit in Celsius?

100°F equals approximately 37.8°C.
