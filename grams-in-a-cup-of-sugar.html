Frequently Asked Questions

How many grams in a cup of sugar?

One cup of granulated white sugar is approximately 200 grams. Brown sugar is 220g and powdered sugar is 120g per cup.

How many grams in half a cup of sugar?

Half a cup of granulated sugar is approximately 100 grams.

How many grams in a cup of brown sugar?

One cup of packed brown sugar is approximately 220 grams.

How many grams in a cup of powdered sugar?

One cup of powdered (confectioners) sugar is approximately 120 grams.

Is a cup of sugar 200g or 225g?

It depends on the source! Most standard references use 200g per cup for granulated sugar. The variation comes from how the sugar is measured — spooned vs scooped into the cup.
