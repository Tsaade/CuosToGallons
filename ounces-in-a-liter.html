Frequently Asked Questions

How many ounces in a liter?

There are 33.81 fluid ounces in 1 liter.

How many ounces in 2 liters?

There are 67.63 fluid ounces in 2 liters.

How many cups in a liter?

There are approximately 4.23 cups in 1 liter.

How many liters in a gallon?

There are 3.785 liters in 1 US gallon, which equals 128 fluid ounces.

Is 16.9 fl oz the same as 500ml?

Yes! 16.9 fl oz is exactly 500ml, which is half a liter. This is the standard size of most bottled water.
