Quick tip: 250ml is almost exactly 1 cup (1.06 cups). Most international recipes that call for 250ml can be treated as 1 cup without any noticeable difference.

US Cups vs Metric

The US cup (237ml) is the standard used in American recipes. Australia and Canada use a metric cup of 250ml, while the UK rarely uses cups at all, preferring milliliters and grams.

Why does this matter?

If you're following a recipe from Australia or Canada, their "1 cup" = 250ml, not 237ml. For most recipes the 13ml difference won't matter, but for precise baking it's worth noting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many ml in a cup of water?

237 ml. Water, milk, juice — any liquid follows the same conversion. 1 US cup = 237 ml.

Is 250ml equal to 1 cup?

Almost. 250ml equals 1.06 US cups. In practice they're close enough to use interchangeably in most recipes.

How many ml in 2 cups?

473 ml. Just multiply 237 by 2.

How many cups is 100ml?

100ml is approximately 0.42 cups, or just under half a cup.

How many ml in a cup of milk?

237 ml. Milk measures the same as water by volume.

How many cups is 500ml?

500ml is approximately 2.11 US cups, or just over 2 cups.
